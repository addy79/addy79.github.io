Ifrane / Morocco

I architect cyber-physical systems, digital twins, and autonomous robotics hardware designed to bridge the chasm between mathematical simulation and rugged physical reality. Working across the full mechatronic stack—from parametric mechanism design (custom cycloidal reducers, harmonic drives, parallel manipulators) to real-time embedded firmware (Raspberry Pi Zero, bare-metal C++, UDP telemetry) and high-fidelity virtual simulation in Godot 4 & OpenXR.

My engineering philosophy centers on first-principles derivation: designing transmissions from involute and cycloidal kinematics, establishing low-latency hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) telemetry links, and validating dynamic multi-body physics before committing to physical fabrication. Whether optimizing actuator control loops or authoring interactive VR engineering teardowns, I build robust, zero-to-one cyber-physical systems.

Technical Skills

Full-stack mechatronic competencies spanning kinematic formulation, physical fabrication, real-time embedded networking, and immersive spatial simulation.

Digitwin1: Hardware-in-the-Loop Cyber-Physical Digital Twin for High-Precision Robotic Actuation
Asmae El Harti, Supervised by Prof. Rachid Lghoul
Al Akhawayn University in Ifrane • School of Science & Engineering Technical Proposal (2024)
View Proposal
This work presents an integrated cyber-physical digital twin architecture for precision 1-DOF robotic actuators. By coupling an OpenXR spatial simulation in Godot 4 with a physical Raspberry Pi Zero micro-controller daemon over bidirectional asynchronous UDP sockets, the system achieves sub-millisecond synchronization, real-time safety interlocks, and dynamic state visualization.
