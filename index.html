Ifrane, Morocco

I am a General Engineering student specializing in Mechatronics at Al Akhawayn University in Ifrane (Class of 2027).

I don't just design mechanical CAD or write simulation scripts in isolation—I build where they collide. My passion lies in taking a complex, multidisciplinary problem and engineering a tangible, working solution from scratch: mathematically deriving epicyclic tooth profiles for zero-backlash reducers, flashing firmware onto microcontrollers, and synchronizing physical hardware telemetry into spatial virtual reality in real-time.

From underwater competition submersibles to classical self-balancing rovers, micromouse maze solvers, and parametric kinematic mechanisms, I engineer machines that move, think, and endure.
