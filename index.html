Ifrane, Morocco

I am a General Engineering student specializing in Mechatronics at Al Akhawayn University in Ifrane and a Software Engineering Scholar at ALX Africa.

I don't just design mechanical CAD or write simulation scripts in isolation—I build where they collide. My passion lies in taking a complex, multidisciplinary problem and engineering a tangible, working solution from scratch. From mathematically deriving the epicyclic tooth profiles of custom cycloidal reducers, to flashing bare-metal telemetry daemons onto a Raspberry Pi Zero, to synchronizing physical hardware telemetry into high-fidelity OpenXR spatial worlds in real-time.

Over the years, I have actively developed hundreds of projects across games, autonomous robotics, parametric mechanism design, and spatial simulation. Whether for industry engineering roles, cutting-edge research labs, or startup collaborations, I bring relentless curiosity and end-to-end execution.
