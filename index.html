I am a General Engineering student specializing in Mechatronics at Al Akhawayn University in Ifrane (Class of 2027).

I don't just design mechanical CAD or write simulation scripts in isolation—I build where they collide. My passion lies in taking a complex, multidisciplinary problem and engineering a tangible, working solution from scratch: mathematically deriving epicyclic tooth profiles for zero-backlash reducers, flashing firmware onto microcontrollers, and synchronizing physical hardware telemetry into spatial virtual reality in real-time.

From underwater competition submersibles to classical self-balancing rovers, micromouse maze solvers, and parametric kinematic mechanisms, I engineer machines that move, think, and endure.

The Obsession Timeline

From first curiosity to building full-scale cyber-physical systems.

Early & Games Era

~Age 4 – 10

Started exploring computational logic at age 4. Built maze and puzzle games, text-based interactive adventures, custom physics platformers, and algorithmic games.

Robotics & Electronics Era

~Age 12 – 20

Stepped into physical hardware at 12: engineered autonomous line-followers, ultrasonic obstacle rovers, and custom sensor rigs. Built classical mechatronics milestones including two-wheeled self-balancing inverted pendulums (Kalman filter/PID), micromouse maze solvers (Flood-Fill algorithm), 4-DOF SCARA manipulators, and 12-DOF quadruped walking gaits. Currently serving as Team Leader & Mechatronics Engineer for the Pioneer ROV underwater exploration submersible competing in this year's marine robotics challenge.

XR & Spatial Simulation Era

~Age 21 – 22

Entered the spatial computing realm: architected virtual reality training laboratories in Godot and OpenXR. Built VRMechanics for 6-DOF mechanical assembly teardowns, BrushlessLab for 3-phase BLDC diagnostic characterization, and VRFields for GPU-accelerated Maxwell electromagnetic vector field visualization.

Godot & OpenXR VRMechanics BrushlessLab VRFields Shaders Spatial UI WebXR

The Cyber-Physical Bridge

~Age 23 – Present

Unifying hardware and virtual reality into seamless cyber-physical systems. Developed Digitwin1: streaming real-time actuator telemetry from physical embedded hardware into an OpenXR simulation over sub-millisecond UDP sockets. Simultaneously engineered precision parametric mechanical mechanisms in FreeCAD (cycloidal drives, harmonic reducers, parallel robots).
