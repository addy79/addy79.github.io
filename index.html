Ifrane, Morocco

I am a General Engineering student specializing in Mechatronics at Al Akhawayn University in Ifrane (Class of 2027) and a Software Engineering Scholar at ALX Africa. My work bridges mechanical engineering, embedded hardware, and high-fidelity physics simulation into unified cyber-physical systems.

I operate across the entire mechatronic lifecycle: deriving first-principles kinematic equations, engineering custom transmissions (zero-backlash cycloidal reducers, harmonic drives, and parallel Stewart/Delta robots in FreeCAD), flashing real-time firmware to microcontrollers and single-board computers (Raspberry Pi Zero, bare-metal C/C++, low-latency UDP bridges), and validating dynamic behavior in immersive spatial environments using Godot 4 and OpenXR.

I am actively seeking internships, graduate research opportunities, and technical founding collaborations where I can build impactful systems across the mechatronics and software stack.

Technical Skills

First-principles mechatronics engineering combining mechanical design, low-level firmware, control theory, and spatial simulation.

The Body: Mechatronics & Hardware

Precision Mechanical Design & CAD

Author of 44 complex parametric assemblies in FreeCAD: custom cycloidal gearboxes, zero-backlash harmonic drives, planetary gearboxes, Stewart hexapod platforms, 6-DOF robotic arms, and Delta parallel robots.

Embedded Systems & Telemetry

Firmware in C/C++ and Python for Raspberry Pi Zero, Arduino, and STM32. Low-latency UDP sockets, UART, SPI, and I2C protocols for sub-millisecond hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) telemetry.

Actuators, Power & Bench Testing

3-phase BLDC outrunners, servomotors, dynamometer test stand integration, milliohm winding resistance characterization, back-EMF waveform analysis, and Hall sensor timing.

Rapid Prototyping & Marine Robotics

FDM and resin 3D printing tolerances, press-fits, bearing integration, and underwater ROV chassis engineering with the AUI Robotics Club.

The Brains: Autonomy & Simulation

Cyber-Physical Digital Twins

Hardware-in-the-loop synchronization in Godot 4, real-time angular position/torque feedback, dynamic joint limits, and emergency hardware interlocks over UDP.

Kinematics & Control Systems

Forward and Inverse Kinematics (FK/IK) for serial and parallel manipulators, PID loop tuning, trajectory generation, and state-space regulation.

Spatial VR & GPU Physics

OpenXR virtual reality training laboratories (VRMechanics, CoAssembly), real-time Maxwell electromagnetic vector field shaders, and particle advection in 3D.
