Digitwin1: Hardware-in-the-Loop Cyber-Physical Digital Twin for High-Precision Robotic Actuation
Asmae El Harti, Supervised by Prof. Rachid Lghoul
Al Akhawayn University in Ifrane • School of Science & Engineering Technical Research Proposal (2024)
Download Proposal Code
This research presents an integrated cyber-physical digital twin architecture for precision 1-DOF robotic actuators. By coupling an OpenXR spatial simulation in Godot 4 with a physical Raspberry Pi Zero micro-controller daemon over bidirectional asynchronous UDP sockets, the system achieves sub-millisecond synchronization, real-time safety interlocks, and dynamic state visualization. We demonstrate how physical sensor drift, encoder quantization, and actuator backlash can be identified in simulation before physical deployment.

Pioneer ROV: Autonomous Underwater Vehicle Design & Regional Competition Strategy
Asmae El Harti, AUI Robotics Club Marine Engineering Division
Al Akhawayn University Robotics Club Technical Report & Regional Strategy (2024)
Download Report
This comprehensive technical proposal details the engineering design of Pioneer ROV, an underwater robotic vehicle tailored for the regional marine challenge. We document the electrical power distribution architecture, 6-thruster vector allocation matrix, sensor telemetry protocol over RS-485/Ethernet tether, and the custom buoyant chassis design optimized for zero pitch/roll drift under variable hydrodynamic drag.

CoAssembly & VRMechanics: Multi-User Networked Spatial Environments for Mechanical Assembly Pedagogy
Asmae El Harti
Virtual Reality & Spatial Computing in Engineering Education (2024)
Download Proposal Code
We propose a multi-user collaborative virtual reality environment built in Godot 4 that enables distributed engineering students and practitioners to simultaneously disassemble, inspect, and evaluate multi-part mechanical mechanisms. By combining real-time spatial networking with high-accuracy rigid body physics, the platform reduces cognitive friction in understanding internal gear meshing, bearing tolerances, and component constraints.

First-Principles Parametric Synthesis of Zero-Backlash Cycloidal and Strain-Wave Gear Reducers in FreeCAD
Asmae El Harti
Open-Source Mechanical Engineering Design Series (2024)
FreeCAD Suite
This technical document provides the mathematical equations and CAD modeling methodologies for generating cycloidal pin gear mechanisms and harmonic flexsplines in open-source CAD (FreeCAD). We analyze the influence of eccentricity, rolling pin diameter, and profile modification coefficients on contact stress, torque transmission ripple, and 3D printing manufacturing tolerances.
